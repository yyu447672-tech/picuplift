    GOOD TO KNOW

    A few clear answers.

    Will my PNG lose quality as a JPG?

    Yes, a little — JPG is lossy, so some detail is discarded. At 90% quality the difference is usually invisible on photos. Your original PNG stays untouched, and you can re-export at another quality any time.

    What happens to transparent parts of a PNG?

    They become white. JPG has no transparency channel, so the tool fills a white background before encoding. If you need to keep transparency, convert to PNG instead — this page only outputs JPG.

    Will the JPG be smaller than my PNG?

    Usually, yes. PNG stores photos losslessly and often ends up large; the same photo as a JPG is usually much smaller. The page shows the real before-and-after size for each file — no specific saving is promised.

    Can I convert several PNG files at once?

    Yes. Add multiple PNG files and they are processed one at a time. Each JPG can be downloaded on its own, or all successful results as one ZIP.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Decoding and conversion run locally in your browser — no uploads, no signup, no account. Files stay on your device.
