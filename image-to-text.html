GOOD TO KNOW

A few clear answers.

How accurate is the text recognition?

Accuracy depends on the image: clear, well-lit printed English reads best, while blurry, tiny, handwritten, or stylized text can be misread, so check the text before use.

Which languages can it read?

This version reads English only. More languages may be added later; other languages produce unreliable results here today.

What kinds of images work best?

Photos and screenshots of printed text work best: documents, signs, book pages, and receipts. Handwriting, very small text, and low-contrast images read poorly.

Can I read text from several images at once?

Yes. Add several images and each gets its own text box. Copy or download each result as .txt, or save all completed results in one ZIP.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. The OCR engine runs locally in your browser, without uploads, signup, or an account. Your images stay on your device.
