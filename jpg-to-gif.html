    GOOD TO KNOW

    A few clear answers.

    How many photos can I put in one GIF?

    Up to 30 photos. The first photo sets the frame size, and later photos are center-cropped to fit it — nothing is stretched. If you add more than 30, only the first 30 are used. For a longer animation, split the photos into several GIFs.

    Can I make a GIF from a single photo?

    Yes. One photo makes a static GIF with a single frame — useful when you need the GIF format itself, for example for a service that only accepts GIF uploads. Add more photos to make it animated.

    Will my photos look the same as a GIF?

    No — GIF holds at most 256 colors, so photos lose smooth gradients and can look flat or banded. Logos, icons, screenshots, and simple graphics convert best. Your original photos stay untouched, and you can re-export with another delay any time.

    Does the GIF keep transparency?

    No — JPG photos have no transparency, so the GIF is fully opaque. This page only accepts JPG files, so transparency never enters the GIF.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Decoding and GIF encoding run locally in your browser — no uploads, no signup, no account. Files stay on your device.
