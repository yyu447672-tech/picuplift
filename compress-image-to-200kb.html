Conversion settings

Automatic: JPG → JPG, PNG → WebP, WebP → WebP. WebP can keep transparency; browser support is checked.

JPG cannot keep transparency. Transparent areas become white.

By quality controls encoding quality only. By target size searches for a file at or below a KB limit; it does not promise an exact byte count.

1 KB = 1024 bytes. The target is an upper limit, not an exact byte count. Quality stays at 40% or higher; dimensions may shrink to 50% of the selected size.

PNG is converted to JPG in target-size mode. JPG or WebP output. PNG inputs always become JPG; transparent areas become white.

    GOOD TO KNOW

    A few clear answers.

    Will a 200KB image still look good?

    Most photos can still look good at 200KB. The tool lowers quality only when needed and shows the final quality and dimensions; preview the image before downloading.

    What formats can I compress to 200KB?

    You can choose JPG, PNG, or WebP inputs. In target-size mode PNG becomes JPG, with transparent areas filled white.

    Can I compress several images to 200KB at once?

    Yes. Add several images and each one is processed separately against the 200KB target.

    What if my image cannot reach 200KB?

    You receive the smallest file the tool can achieve within its quality and size limits. The result clearly shows its actual size, quality, and dimensions.

    Does my file leave my browser?

    No. All image processing happens locally in your browser, with no upload or signup.
