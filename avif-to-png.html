    GOOD TO KNOW

    A few clear answers.

    Does PNG keep the transparency of my AVIF?

    Yes. PNG keeps the alpha channel, so transparent areas stay transparent and semi-transparent edges stay soft. If the AVIF has no transparency, the PNG is fully opaque.

    Will the PNG be smaller than my AVIF?

    Usually not. AVIF compresses photos far more efficiently than PNG, which stores images losslessly. The page shows the real before-and-after size for each file — no specific saving is promised.

    Which browsers can convert AVIF here?

    Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari can decode AVIF locally. If your browser cannot decode a file, its result row shows a clear error, and the file is still never uploaded.

    Can I convert several AVIF files at once?

    Yes. Add several AVIFs and they are processed one at a time. Download each PNG individually or all successful results in one ZIP.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Decoding and conversion happen locally in your browser — no uploads, no signup, no account. Files stay on your device.
