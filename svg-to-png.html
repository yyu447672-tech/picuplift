    GOOD TO KNOW

    A few clear answers.

    Does SVG to PNG preserve transparency?

    Yes. Transparent is the default background and PNG keeps alpha. A background rectangle already drawn inside your SVG remains part of the picture. White or a custom color fills only transparent areas.

    How is the default export size chosen?

    The first SVG’s width and height are used, including absolute units converted to pixels. Otherwise its viewBox is used; if neither gives a usable size, the page shows a 512 × 512 px fallback. The chosen dimensions apply to the whole batch.

    Why can text look different?

    SVG text uses fonts installed on your device. A missing font can change the result. Convert text to paths in a vector editor before exporting a logo that must look consistent.

    Can my SVG use external images or fonts?

    No. External images, fonts and stylesheets are rejected, along with foreignObject and animation. Use self-contained paths and shapes. The tool does not fetch resources referenced inside an SVG.

    Can an SVG script run on this page?

    No. SVG source is never inserted into the page. A detached XML document is read only for validation and dimensions; rendering uses an Image loaded from a Blob URL, where SVG scripts do not execute.

    Why is PNG larger, and are there limits?

    PNG stores raster pixels, so a logo may be larger than its SVG source. Reduce export dimensions or compress the PNG. Exports are limited to 8,192 px per side and 16,777,216 pixels total to protect memory. Browser and ZIP capacity can impose lower limits.
