# WebP to JPG sample photograph

The opaque cat photograph is the scikit-image **Chelsea** sample, photographed by **Stefan van der Walt** and released under **CC0**. The scikit-image data documentation states there are no copyright restrictions for this photograph.

Development source (not contacted by the website):
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scikit-image/scikit-image/v0.18.3/skimage/data/chelsea.png

Copyright/provenance documentation:
https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.chelsea

PicUplift locally encoded the source as WebP with browser Canvas, then generated the JPG using the WebP-to-JPG tool at quality90 and original dimensions. The before and after files are real format conversions, not generated photographs. The demo wrapper and original WebP sample carry identical WebP bytes. No image is fetched from the source URLs at runtime.
