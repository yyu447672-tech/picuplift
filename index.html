    Private by design. Useful by default.

    PicUplift gives everyday image tasks a simpler home. Convert a phone photo, keep a transparent background, or make an image lighter—all on your own device.

    Every tool on this page runs entirely in your browser. Your photos are decoded, converted, and compressed on your own device — nothing is uploaded to a server, so there is no waiting room, no account, and no copy of your images left behind. HEIC files from your iPhone become JPGs or PNGs that open anywhere; WebP images keep their transparency when they become PNGs; and large JPGs, PNGs, and WebP files shrink to a size that is easier to share or upload. Quality, size, and output format are yours to adjust before you convert, and you can download results one by one or together as a ZIP. The same workflow works on Windows, Mac, and Linux, in any current browser — and because the site is a static download, you can even use it offline. No installation is needed, and there is nothing to update: open the page, pick a tool, and your images stay yours from start to finish.

    GOOD TO KNOW

    A few clear answers.

    Are all tools free?

    Yes. All tools are free, with no registration, conversion quotas, or advertisements.

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. Your images stay in this browser tab. We use Google Analytics to measure visits and interactions (such as page views, scrolling, and clicks). Images are always processed locally and are never uploaded.

    Are there file-size or quantity limits?

    PicUplift sets no artificial file-size or conversion-count caps. Available memory, image dimensions, browser decoders, and ZIP format capacity still impose practical limits. Large batches are processed sequentially.

    Can I use it without an internet connection?

    Yes. Download the full project folder and open index.html in a current desktop Chrome or Edge. Keep assets and licenses alongside the HTML pages. No installation or build is required.

    What happens to animated pictures and metadata?

    Converted outputs are static images, except JPG to GIF and PNG to GIF, which make animated GIFs. An animated WebP, PNG, GIF, or AVIF uses its first frame, and a multi-image HEIC uses its primary image. Converted outputs do not copy camera metadata. Originals stay untouched.

    Is PicUplift related to PromptUplift?

    Yes. PicUplift follows the same simple, free-tool approach and visual language as PromptUplift. This site focuses on everyday image tasks.
