    GOOD TO KNOW

    A few clear answers.

    Will my photo lose quality as a WebP?

    Yes, a little — WebP is lossy, so some detail is discarded. At 90% quality the difference is usually invisible on photos. Your original file stays untouched, and you can re-export at another quality any time.

    Does WebP keep transparency?

    Yes. Transparent areas of a PNG stay transparent in WebP, so logos keep working on any background. JPG files have no transparency, so their WebP output is fully opaque.

    How much smaller will the WebP be?

    Usually much smaller. WebP compresses photos far better than PNG and usually beats JPG too. The page shows the real before-and-after size for each file — no specific saving is promised.

    Can I convert PNG and JPG files together?

    Yes. Add PNG and JPG files in one batch and they are processed one at a time. Each WebP can be downloaded on its own, or all successful results as one ZIP.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Decoding and conversion run locally in your browser — no uploads, no signup, no account. Files stay on your device.
